RULES

  • Text on orange is always navy, never white.
  • One orange primary action per screen, at the bottom.
  • Touch targets are at least 44 px.
  • Prices in Kz with a thousands comma: 4,200 Kz.
  • Status uses color plus a word, never color alone.
